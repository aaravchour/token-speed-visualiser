Why Speed Matters

The "Reading" Threshold

The average adult reads silently at about 5-8 tokens per second (238 words per minute). When an AI generates text slower than this (e.g., 2-3 tk/s), the user feels "lag" and impatience. This is why early chatbot models felt sluggish.

Once generation hits 10-15 tk/s (like GPT-4), it feels "conversational" because the text appears roughly at the speed you can process it comfortably.

Beyond Human Perception

At speeds of 50+ tk/s, the text generates faster than anyone can read. This is critical for non-chat applications, such as:

  • Code Generation: Filling entire files instantly.
  • Data Extraction: Processing large documents in milliseconds.
  • Voice Assistants: Reducing latency to make speech sound natural.
